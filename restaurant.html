Odoo for Restaurants · set up by TechNext

One system for the whole eatery.

Bookings, floor, kitchen and stock — TechNext ties Odoo's restaurant apps together so Auntie Gaik Lean runs a full house from a single platform, from the first reservation to the last bill.

Fire orders straight to the kitchen

Counter and kitchen display, working as one. Servers work the floor plan table by table, build the Nyonya tasting combos in a tap, and every ticket lands in the kitchen the moment it's rung up — no shouting, no lost slips.

Let guests book their own table 🙂

Reservations that manage themselves. Guests book online, and every reservation drops straight onto your floor plan — which your host rearranges with a simple drag-and-drop, so there are no double-booked tables on a Sunday rush.

Keep the pantry stocked

No running dry mid-service. Auto-reorder the trackable staples and keep recurring tasks for the market-day items, then create, track and confirm purchase orders so stock levels are always current.

Roster the team like a pro

Shifts sorted, everyone informed. Plan front-of-house and kitchen shifts, assign them automatically or tweak by hand around who's free, cover every role, and share the line-up so tomorrow's team knows exactly when they're on.
